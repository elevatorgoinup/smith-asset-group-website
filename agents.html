How the split actually works

There's no fixed public rate card here on purpose — every referral fee or co-broke split is negotiated per deal and put in writing before any client information changes hands, consistent with Georgia real estate license law (a referral fee to a licensed agent or broker is standard practice; nothing here is structured to pay an unlicensed party for activity that requires a license).

  • Seller referral: you introduce the seller, we handle the acquisition and paperwork, your fee is paid at closing per the written referral agreement.
  • Buyer-side representation: your buyer client purchases an assigned contract or closes with us directly; your commission follows the agreement you already have with that buyer.
  • Nothing is exclusive. Working with us on one deal doesn't obligate you (or us) on the next one — we'd just rather build a relationship than a one-off.

This page describes a general framework, not a binding offer or a specific commission rate. Terms for any individual deal are set out in a separate written referral or co-brokerage agreement reviewed by both parties.
