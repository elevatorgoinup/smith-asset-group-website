Privacy Policy

Effective & last updated: September 18, 2026

01Smith Asset Group LLC ("Smith Asset Group," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information when you visit this website or communicate with us by phone, text (SMS), or email.

Information we collect

We may collect the following information when you contact us or submit a form on this site:

  • Contact details: name, phone number, email address, and mailing address
  • Property details: address, condition, and other information you provide about a property
  • Communications: records of calls, texts, and emails exchanged with us

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18, and this site is not directed at minors.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Evaluate and respond to property, buyer, or partnership inquiries
  • Contact you by phone, text message, or email about your inquiry
  • Prepare offers, agreements, and closing documents when a deal moves forward
  • Maintain internal records of leads, buyers, and transactions

Text messaging (SMS) & mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

If you provide your mobile number, you may receive text messages from Smith Asset Group LLC related to your property or investment inquiry, including by automated means. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. You may reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages, or HELP for assistance. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • With title companies, closing attorneys, or transaction partners as needed to complete a specific real estate transaction you are party to
  • With service providers who support our business operations (such as our CRM or communications platform), bound to keep your information confidential
  • When required by law, subpoena, or legal process

Data retention & security

We retain your information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy or as required by law. We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information, though no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and you may ask us to stop calling or emailing you by contacting us directly using the information below. You may also request that we delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to any legal recordkeeping requirements.

Cookies & website data

This website may use basic cookies or analytics to understand how visitors use the site. This information is used in aggregate and is not used to identify you personally.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of this site or continued communication with us after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
