Privacy Policy

Effective & last updated: October 21, 2026

Smith Asset Group LLC ("Smith Asset Group," "we," "us," or "our") is a Georgia real estate investment and wholesaling company based in Augusta, Georgia. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain terms, what personal information we collect, where it comes from, which outside companies help us handle it, how long we keep it, and how you can see, correct, or delete it. It covers this website (smithassetgrp.com), our members app (the Living Room, at smith-asset-group-living-room.onrender.com), and our phone calls, text messages, and emails.

The short version: we do not sell your personal information, we do not share your phone number for anyone else's marketing, and you can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing smithassetgrp@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us when you fill out a form (sell your house, buyer "First Look" list, ask about a property, agent referral, Vault document request), call, text, or email us:

  • Contact details: name, phone number, and email address
  • Property details: the property address, beds, baths, square footage, year built, condition, asking price, and anything else you tell us about it
  • Buyer preferences: price range, target areas, and minimum beds or baths
  • Your text-message consent choice
  • Records of our calls, texts, and emails with you

If you create a Living Room account: your username, email address, the role you pick (wholesaler, cash buyer, agent, lender, contractor, or other), an optional bio, and the chat messages you post. Your password is stored only as a one-way scrambled code (a bcrypt hash), so we cannot see it. A login cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days.

Information collected automatically on the website (see "Analytics" below): pages viewed, buttons and links clicked, which neighborhoods, streets, and properties are looked up on the map, page speed, browser and device type, and your approximate city and region based on your internet connection.

Information from public records and data providers: to find properties that may be for sale, we use public county records (such as owner name, mailing address, assessed value, and sale history from Augusta-Richmond County) and data providers that supply owner contact information (skip tracing). If you are a property owner and want us to stop contacting you or remove your contact details, tell us and we will.

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and this site is not directed at minors. Please do not send us sensitive information such as Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, or health information through the website.

How we use your information

  • To evaluate and respond to property, buyer, agent, or partnership inquiries
  • To contact you by phone, text, or email about your inquiry
  • To prepare offers, agreements, and closing documents when a deal moves forward
  • To send buyers on our First Look list properties that match what they asked for
  • To run the Living Room app: logging you in, showing your profile, the chat, and password-reset emails
  • To keep records of leads, buyers, and transactions
  • To understand how the website is used and make it faster and more useful
  • To protect the site and app from fraud, spam, and misuse, and to meet legal obligations

Use of artificial intelligence (AI)

We use AI tools, including Claude by Anthropic, to help run our business: for example to organize and summarize leads and property information, analyze comparable sales, draft messages and documents that a person at Smith Asset Group reviews, and build and maintain this website. Information you send us may be processed by these tools for those purposes.

We do not use AI to make automatic decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Every offer, contract, and decision about a deal is made by a person. This website does not currently include an AI chatbot. If we add one, we will say so on the page where it appears, and it will direct anyone who mentions self-harm or a crisis to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988).

Text messaging (SMS) & mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

If you provide your mobile number, you may receive text messages from Smith Asset Group LLC related to your property or investment inquiry, including by automated means. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency varies. You may reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages, or HELP for assistance. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Companies that help us (service providers)

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for other companies' advertising. We use the following outside services to run the website, the app, and the business. Each receives only what it needs to do its job:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): delivers the website forms you fill out to our email inbox
  • Google: Gmail stores our business email, including form submissions and password-reset emails. Google Fonts supplies the fonts on this site, so your browser connects to Google's servers when a page loads.
  • PostHog (US servers): website analytics (see below)
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): hosts this website. Like any web host, it may log visitors' internet addresses for security.
  • Render: runs the Living Room app
  • MongoDB: the database that stores Living Room accounts, chat messages, and our internal lead notes
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs): supplies the open-source map software (Leaflet) that runs in your browser
  • Augusta-Richmond County GIS (gismap.augustaga.gov) and Esri (server.arcgisonline.com): when you use the property map, your browser requests parcel records, sales, aerial photos, and road maps directly from these public map servers. The address or street you search is sent to them as part of that request.
  • Anthropic (Claude): AI tools described above
  • CRM, automation, and data providers we use to manage leads and find property owners, such as ReSimpli (CRM), Zapier (moves form entries into our CRM), and DealMachine (property and owner data)

We also share information with title companies, closing attorneys, lenders, buyers, and other parties to a specific real estate transaction you are part of, as needed to complete it; with a buyer or successor if our business is sold or reorganized; and when required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect our rights or someone's safety. We do not publish sellers' names or contact information on our deal listings.

Analytics

This website uses PostHog analytics, which sets a cookie in your browser, to count visits and see which pages, buttons, map searches, neighborhoods, streets, and properties people use. Map search events record whether you searched an address, street, or parcel number, not the words you typed. We do not record what you type into forms, we do not record your screen (session recording is turned off), and we do not use analytics for advertising. Heat maps show where on a page people click, in aggregate.

If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, our analytics does not load at all. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the site will still work.

How long we keep information

  • Leads and inquiries (form submissions, calls, texts, emails): kept while they are useful for working with you and for our business records, and deleted when you ask us to
  • Closed transactions: contracts and closing records are kept for at least 7 years for tax and legal recordkeeping
  • Living Room accounts and chat messages: kept until you delete your account. You can delete it yourself on your Profile page, which immediately removes your account, your profile, and every chat message you posted, and signs you out.
  • Password-reset links: expire after 1 hour (24 hours for a link our team sends you)
  • Website analytics: kept according to PostHog's retention settings for our account
  • Text-message opt-outs: we keep a record of your STOP request so we do not text you again

Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we have about you and give you a copy
  • Correct information that is wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Stop calling, texting, or emailing you (or reply STOP to any text)

Email smithassetgrp@gmail.com or call (762) 215-4466. We may need to confirm it is really you, for example by replying from the email or phone number we have on file. We will respond within 45 days. When we delete your information, we remove it from our email, CRM, app, and lead records, and tell the service providers above to do the same where they hold a copy. We may keep only what the law requires (such as closed-transaction records) or a minimal note that you asked not to be contacted, so that we honor that request. We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights.

Residents of Georgia, California, and other states with consumer privacy laws may have these rights under state law. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the word "Appeal," and we will review it again within 45 days.

Security

The website and app use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Living Room passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes, login sessions use secure, http-only cookies, and repeated failed logins are rate-limited. Access to lead records is limited to our team. We do not store credit card or bank account numbers. Vault payments are made through Cash App, PayPal, or Zelle, and those companies handle the payment details. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page shows the most recent revision. If we make a significant change to how we use your information, we will post it here before it takes effect and, where we have your email and the change affects you, tell you directly.
