Seller questions

Still unsure? Call or text (762) 215-4466
How do you come up with your offer?

We look at what similar homes nearby have sold for, what the house needs, and what it costs us to buy, hold, and resell it. We walk you through the math so you can see how we got there.

Will your offer be lower than listing with an agent?

Usually, yes. A cash offer is typically below full retail value. In exchange you skip repairs, showings, commissions, and months of waiting, and you get a firm closing date. If listing makes more sense for you, we'll tell you.

Do I have to make repairs or clean out the house?

No. Sell it as-is. Take what you want and leave the rest.

Are there any fees or commissions?

We don't charge you fees or commissions. We'll go over who pays which closing costs in writing before you sign anything.

How fast can you close? Can I pick a later date?

Many deals can close in a few weeks once title is clear. If you need more time to move, you pick the date.

Can I sell with tenants, liens, back taxes, or code violations?

Often, yes. These situations are common, and the closing attorney sorts out payoffs and title. Tell us what's going on and we'll be straight with you about your options.

What if I inherited the house or it's in probate?

We work with inherited properties regularly. If probate isn't finished yet, we can still talk and plan around your attorney's timeline.

Will you sell or assign the contract to another buyer?

Sometimes. We may close on the house ourselves or assign our contract to one of our investor buyers. Either way your price and terms stay the same, and we'll tell you up front.
